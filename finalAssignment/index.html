About the Series

My Hero Academia, also known as Boku No Hero Academia in Japan is an extremely popular anime/manga about a young quirkless boy who aspired to be the world's greatest hero. The anime was adapted by Bones Studio and written by Kohei Horikoshi.

Main Characters

  • Izuku Midoriya
  • Katsuki Bakugo
  • Shoto Todoroki
  • Ochaco Uraraka
